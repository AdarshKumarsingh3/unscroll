# 🌊 UNSCROLL: Anti-Doomscroll Platform
## Executive MVP Presentation & Founder Deliverable

> **Core Value Proposition**: *"Friction + Replacement + Community — Reclaiming human attention from algorithmic feeds."*

---

### Executive Overview

Traditional website blockers fail because they rely purely on conscious willpower. When an urge strikes and a blocker presents an empty locked screen, the neurochemical dopamine craving remains unsatisfied, provoking instant uninstalls or bypasses.

**UNSCROLL** solves this through a defensible 3-pillar loop:
1. **Friction (Reality-Check Interceptor)**: Injects a calm 5-second mindfulness pause before the feed opens, breaking automatic thumb habits.
2. **Replacement (30-90s Micro-Bites)**: Instantly fulfills the underlying emotional driver (boredom, stress, loneliness) with satisfying mini-activities.
3. **Community (Shared Presence)**: Live Focus Rooms, public Saved-Hours Walls, and friend streaks convert individual struggle into collective pride.

### 📅 The 90-Day Execution Model & Phase Gates

#### Phase 1: Foundation & Viral Entry (Days 1–30)
* **Week 1**: Brand & user interviews, landing page with waitlist.
* **Week 2**: Viral Screen-Time Cost Calculator with shareable result cards.
* **Week 3**: Urge Surfer module & first 10 micro-activities.
* **Week 4**: Public community launch & content calendar.
* **Gate 1**: $\ge$ 500 waitlist signups or calculator completions.

#### Phase 2: Retention Core (Days 31–60)
* **Week 5**: User accounts, onboarding trigger selection, dashboard.
* **Week 6**: Chrome & Firefox extension (Manifest V3) in beta.
* **Week 7**: Expand activity library to 30+ items, closed beta (50–100 users).
* **Week 8**: Fix top beta issues, submit extension to Web Store.
* **Gate 2**: Day-7 retention $\ge$ 25% among beta cohort.

#### Phase 3: Community & Scale (Days 61–90)
* **Week 9**: Live Focus Rooms (shared Pomodoro + ambient audio + counter).
* **Week 10**: Payments integration (Stripe monthly/annual) & 7-day trial.
* **Week 11**: 2 to 3 institutional pilots (university club, bootcamp).
* **Week 12**: Public launch push (Product Hunt, creator partnerships).
